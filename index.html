About

Final-year software engineering student at Toros University, graduating June 2027. I work mainly in C# and .NET, with Python as a second tool. I'm interested in the unglamorous part of software: data models that don't rot, APIs that behave predictably, and systems someone else can pick up after me.

Internal management panel

A family-run security training institution was tracking student records, course groups and certifications on paper and in scattered spreadsheets.
