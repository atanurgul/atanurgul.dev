About

Final-year software engineering student at Toros University, graduating June 2027. I work mainly in C# and .NET, with Python as a second tool. I'm interested in the unglamorous part of software: data models that don't rot, APIs that behave predictably, and systems someone else can pick up after me.

Selected work

01

Yggdrasil

A Norse mythology reader built as a progressive web app: 102 bilingual entries across nine chapters, interlinked, with a glossary and a timeline. The interesting constraint was having no backend at all — everything is structured content shipped to the client, which forced the data model to carry weight the server usually would.

02

Internal management panel

A family-run security training institution was tracking student records, course groups and certifications on paper and in scattered spreadsheets. I built an internal panel around a single source of truth, so enrolment, attendance and certification all read from the same data instead of three copies of it.

03

Institution website

The institution had no online presence in a market where people search before they call. Built as a static promotional site with WhatsApp-first contact, deliberately kept separate from the internal panel so the two could change independently.
